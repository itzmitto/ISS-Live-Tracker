05 / RADAR INFORMATION

Understanding Visibility

Above the horizon

A positive elevation angle means the ISS is geometrically above the observer's horizon.

Below the horizon

A negative elevation angle means Earth blocks the direct line of sight to the ISS.

Seeing the ISS at night

Geometric visibility does not guarantee a visible sighting. Lighting, weather and local obstructions also matter.

All readings are calculated from current ISS coordinates using a spherical Earth model. This is not a radio-tracking receiver or a future-pass prediction service.
