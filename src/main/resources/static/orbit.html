About These Estimates

The calculations assume a circular orbit around a spherical Earth. They use the latest reported ISS altitude and Earth's standard gravitational parameter. The real ISS orbit is not perfectly circular, and orbital maneuvers, atmospheric drag and Earth oblateness affect its motion. Estimates are not precision orbit predictions or official flight dynamics data.
